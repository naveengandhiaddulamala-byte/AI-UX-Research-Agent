===== TOP UX PRIORITY =====

Problem:
Product Selection

Affected users:
4

Percentage:
40%

Why it matters:
Users suffer from choice paralysis due to a high volume of products without helpful navigation or comparison tools. Irrelevant search results and a lack of guiding information make it difficult for users to confidently choose the right products.


===== UX RECOMMENDATION =====

1. Improve Product Selection Guidance: Introduce a clear, side-by-side product comparison tool that allows users to easily evaluate similar products. Optimize search relevance to prevent users from being overwhelmed by irrelevant results, and add filtering options and guides to help them select the best items.
2. Improve Price Transparency: Display complete pricing information, including any additional delivery charges, early in the user flow (e.g., on product pages or cart screens) rather than only at final checkout.
3. Improve Delivery Transparency: Show estimated delivery times prominently on both the homepage/product page and before order confirmation to set clear user expectations.
