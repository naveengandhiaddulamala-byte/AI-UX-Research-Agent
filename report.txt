===== TOP UX PRIORITY =====

Problem:
Product Selection Guidance

Affected users:
4

Percentage:
40%

Why it matters:
Users are overwhelmed by too many choices and irrelevant search results. They lack the guidance and comparison tools necessary to make confident purchasing decisions.


===== UX RECOMMENDATION =====

1. Improve the search algorithm to filter out irrelevant results and offer easy-to-use search filters.
2. Introduce a side-by-side comparison feature for similar products to help users easily compare pricing, sizes, and nutritional information.
3. Incorporate guidance tags (e.g., "Best Value", "Highly Rated") to assist users in making quick, informed selection decisions.
4. Display accurate and upfront product pricing, inclusive of delivery charges, early in the shopping flow (e.g., in the shopping cart) to prevent unexpected final amounts.
5. Make estimated delivery times highly visible on both product and pre-checkout screens, allowing users to know delivery times before they place an order.
