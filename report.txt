===== TOP UX PRIORITY =====

Problem:
Lack of Pricing Transparency and Unexpected Checkout Costs

Affected users:
3

Why it matters:
Hidden fees and discrepancies between product page prices and the final price degrade user trust, create friction, and lead directly to cart abandonment.

===== UX RECOMMENDATION =====

Display delivery fees and total estimated costs transparently on the cart page or product pages before checkout. Ensure that prices displayed on the item pages are fully accurate and consistent with the final billing amount.
